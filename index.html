About me

I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence) at the University of Tehran, advised by Dr. Bagher BabaAli. My research focuses on deep learning for time-series and sensor data, and my thesis studies offline handwritten text modeling with vision-language and diffusion models.

Alongside my studies, I work as a C++ desktop developer and deep learning researcher: I build real-time Qt applications that acquire, process, and visualize sensor signals, and I train deep learning models on this data. In 2025 I was part of the team that won 1st place in the Sussex-Huawei Locomotion Challenge (Task 1).

What I bring is the combination of both sides: solid C++ engineering for real-time systems and modern deep learning research. I am looking for a PhD position starting in 2027.
